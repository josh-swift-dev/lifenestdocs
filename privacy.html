Privacy Policy

LifeNest Docs

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

LifeNest Docs is developed by Joshua Marcum. We respect your privacy and aim to provide a document organizer that keeps your personal information under your control.

This policy explains how LifeNest Docs handles documents, device permissions, purchases, and support communications.

2. Document Storage

Documents, imported files, scanned images, notes, categories, and associated metadata are stored locally within the application's storage on your device.

LifeNest Docs does not require an account or upload your document library to a developer-operated server as part of its normal document management functionality.

Files exported or shared by you may be handled by other applications or storage providers according to their own privacy practices.

3. Text Recognition and Search

LifeNest Docs uses Apple's Vision framework for text recognition. Recognized text is used to help you search your saved documents.

Document text recognition is performed on your device through the application's implemented recognition workflow.

4. Camera and Files Access

Camera access is used when you choose to scan or photograph a document.

File access is used when you choose to import documents, export backups, or restore files.

These permissions and file selections are used to provide the features you request.

5. Face ID and Device Authentication

LifeNest Docs can use Apple's LocalAuthentication framework to protect access to the app.

Biometric verification is managed by iOS. LifeNest Docs does not receive or store your Face ID biometric data.

6. Notifications

If you enable notifications, LifeNest Docs can schedule local reminders for document expiration dates.

You may change notification permissions through your device settings.

7. In-App Purchases

LifeNest Docs offers an optional one-time Pro purchase to unlock unlimited document storage.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. LifeNest Docs uses purchase entitlement information to determine whether Pro features are unlocked.

We do not directly receive your full payment card details through the app.

8. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The current LifeNest Docs application code does not implement third-party advertising SDKs, developer-operated analytics collection, or cross-app advertising tracking.

Apple may independently process App Store purchases, diagnostics, and other platform information under Apple's own policies and your device settings.

9. Backup and Export Security

LifeNest Docs allows you to export a ZIP backup containing your document records and attachments.

Exported ZIP backups are not encrypted or password-protected by LifeNest Docs. Anyone with access to an exported backup may be able to view its contents.

You are responsible for choosing a secure storage location and managing copies of exported documents.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Your documents remain in the app's local storage until you delete them, restore another library, or remove the app and its associated local data.

Deleting a document through the app removes its record and associated managed attachment. Copies you previously exported or shared are not automatically deleted.

Device backups and external storage services may retain copies according to their own settings and policies.

11. Support Communications

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message. We use this information to respond to your support request.

Please avoid emailing sensitive identification, financial, or medical documents unless necessary.

12. Children's Privacy

LifeNest Docs is a general-purpose document organization application and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated account system.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the application evolves. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date when appropriate.
