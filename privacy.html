LifeNest Docs

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

LifeNest Docs is designed to help you organize important personal documents while keeping you in control of your information. This policy explains how the application handles your data.

1. Information You Add

LifeNest Docs allows you to import and organize documents, photographs, document titles, categories, notes, reminders, and other information you choose to provide.

2. Local Document Storage

Documents and related information are stored locally on your device. LifeNest Docs does not require an account or operate a developer-hosted server for uploading your documents.

Your device's operating system and backup settings may affect whether application data is included in device backups.

3. Camera and Photos

With your permission, LifeNest Docs may access your camera or selected photographs so you can add documents. This access is used only to provide the features you request.

4. Face ID and Device Authentication

If you enable application locking, LifeNest Docs uses Apple's device authentication features. The application does not receive or store your Face ID biometric information.

5. Notifications

With your permission, LifeNest Docs may schedule local notifications for document expiration dates and reminders. You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings.

6. In-App Purchases

LifeNest Docs offers an optional lifetime Pro unlock. Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. The developer does not receive your full payment card details.

7. Sharing and Exporting

When you choose to export or share documents, the destination and any subsequent handling of those files depend on the application or service you select.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete documents within the application. Deleting the application generally removes its locally stored data, subject to iOS backup and restoration behavior. Export important documents before deleting the application.

9. Security

LifeNest Docs uses available iOS security features, including optional device authentication. No application or storage method can guarantee absolute security. We recommend keeping your device updated and protected with a strong passcode.

10. Children's Privacy

LifeNest Docs is not specifically designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly solicit their personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application functionality or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page.
