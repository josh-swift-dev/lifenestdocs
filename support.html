Frequently Asked Questions

Is LifeNest Docs free?

Yes. You can save up to 7 documents for free. LifeNest Docs Pro unlocks unlimited documents with a one-time $1.99 purchase. There is no subscription.

How do I unlock unlimited documents?

Open the Settings tab and select the Pro upgrade option. Follow the App Store purchase instructions.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings and select Restore Purchases. Make sure you are using the Apple Account associated with the original purchase.

How do I scan a document?

Tap Add a Document, choose Scan Document to PDF, capture your document, adjust the crop area when prompted, and save your scan. You can combine multiple pages into one PDF.

Can I import existing files?

Yes. LifeNest Docs supports importing PDF files and common image formats, including JPG, PNG, and HEIC.

How do expiration reminders work?

Add an expiration date to a document and choose a reminder interval. Allow notifications in iOS Settings to receive alerts. Notification delivery is subject to your device settings.

Can I search inside scanned documents?

LifeNest Docs uses on-device text recognition to make recognized document text searchable. Recognition accuracy depends on scan quality and document content.

How do I protect my documents?

Enable App Lock in Settings to require Face ID or your device passcode when accessing LifeNest Docs.

How do I back up my documents?

Open Settings and use the backup export option to create a ZIP archive. Save it somewhere secure. Compatible LifeNest Docs backups can be restored through the app.

Are backups encrypted?

No. Exported ZIP backups are not password-protected or encrypted by LifeNest Docs. They may contain sensitive documents. Store them securely and avoid sharing them with untrusted parties.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting the app may permanently remove documents stored locally on your device. Export and safely store a backup before uninstalling or replacing your device.

Troubleshooting

If something isn't working correctly:

  1. Update LifeNest Docs to the latest available version.
  2. Restart the app and your iPhone.
  3. Check camera, notification, and Face ID permissions.
  4. Verify that sufficient device storage is available.
  5. Contact support if the problem continues.

Do not uninstall the app as a troubleshooting step unless you have confirmed that your documents are safely backed up.
